# Product Vision

## Pitch
QuoteDrive helps B2B consulting teams turn structured discovery inputs, configurable service packages, and governed commercial assumptions into polished, reviewable, client-ready proposal drafts.

## Problem
Proposal work often lives across spreadsheets, documents, email, and individual memory. This creates inconsistent scope, weak version history, unclear assumptions, and slow approvals. QuoteDrive makes the workflow explicit: configure a proposal, produce a deterministic illustrative estimate, draft narrative with AI, review, approve, and share.

## Personas
- **Organization Admin:** configures a tenant workspace, catalogue, members, roles, and feature settings.
- **Proposal Manager:** creates customers/opportunities, configures a proposal, drafts text, and submits for approval.
- **Approver:** reviews a stable proposal version and approves or requests changes.
- **Viewer:** reads shared or approved proposals.

## Goals
1. Demonstrate a credible React + FastAPI + PostgreSQL SaaS vertical slice.
2. Keep workflow transitions, permissions, estimates, and audit records deterministic.
3. Make AI provenance, fallback behavior, and human review visible.
4. Deliver a polished generic-consultancy product with a fictional fleet-mobility demo template.

## Non-goals
No real payments, e-signature, CRM integration, external vehicle feed, loan/credit workflow, financial advice, real customer data, or autonomous commercial decision.
