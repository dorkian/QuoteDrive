# Synthetic Data Policy

## Statement
Every organization, customer, contact, vehicle, and price shown in QuoteDrive is fictional or synthetic. QuoteDrive is not a financial institution, lender, credit-scoring system, binding quote engine, legal tool, or compliance product. No real company, real person, or real commercial offer is represented anywhere in the product, demo data, or documentation.

## Rules for adding demo data
- Do not use a real company name, real person's name, or real personal data.
- Do not source pricing from a real vendor, real market rate card, or scraped data. Estimates are illustrative planning figures only.
- Every estimate, preview, or proposal total must carry the disclaimer "Illustrative planning estimate only."
- Do not use a real vehicle brand or model; describe fleet items by category (e.g. "electric city vehicle") rather than a real make/model.

## Canonical example
`docs/product/demo-scenario.md` is the reference demo narrative — reuse its tenant, customer, and fleet scenario rather than inventing a new one.
