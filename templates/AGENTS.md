# QuoteDrive Agent Contract

QuoteDrive is an educational multi-tenant B2B proposal SaaS. It is not a lender, credit-scoring system, binding quote engine, legal tool, or production compliance product.

- Use synthetic data only.
- Never implement creditworthiness, affordability, eligibility, lending, automated commercial approval, or financial decisioning.
- Estimates are illustrative; preserve disclaimers.
- AI drafts supplied facts only; it may not invent prices, discounts, dates, guarantees, terms, or product capability.
- AI output requires human review.
- Tenant-owned operations must be server-scoped by organization ID.
- Workflow transitions are deterministic, server-side, and tested.
- Do not add dependencies, integrations, secrets, migrations, or infrastructure without approval.
- Use FakeProvider for automated AI tests.
- Each task needs acceptance criteria, tests, evidence, and a Completion TL;DR.
